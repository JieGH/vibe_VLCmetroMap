## Xarxa 1.4.0: a new home at xarxa.net

**Open the map: https://xarxa.net.** Live trains now come straight from Metrovalencia's own feed on the website too, so it shows the same trains as the app. The old address (`tangerine-panda-1aeea5.netlify.app`) now just asks visitors to move.

### On the website
- **Language button in the top bar** (EN, ES, VAL): Valencian is new. Next to it, a dark/light button and an Android download button.
- **Pick a line and the map frames it,** showing the main stations first and the rest as you zoom in.
- **The page finds you by itself.** Allow location once and every visit opens on you and your nearest station, zoomed out a little less than before.
- **The walking box** ("3 min on foot · Xàtiva · 180 m") now sits above the station list, counts the distance down as you walk, and stays until you close it.
- **A bigger blue location button,** a roomier menu with a one-line text size control and sun / moon / auto icons, and the author's signature at the bottom.
- **Search shows popular stations and a grid of lines** before you type anything.
- Fixes: map icons on sharp screens, and the map not loading on some browsers.

### Android app (version 1.4.0, new native app)
- **Download: `Xarxa-android.apk`** below. It needs Android 8 or newer. It is not on Google Play yet, so Android asks you to allow the install.
- A new menu with the app icon, language and appearance toggles (English, Spanish, Valencian), and a visible version number.
- Pick a line and the map frames the whole line, main stations first.
- Search shows popular stations and a grid of lines before you type.
- Fixes: a crash on Android 8.0 and 8.1, and a crash when the phone switched between dark and light while the app was open.
- It installs over earlier test builds of the native app (same signature). Obtainium users: add `https://github.com/JieGH/vibe_VLCmetroMap` and set the APK filter to `.*android\.apk`.

### iPhone
No iPhone app yet. Open https://xarxa.net in Safari and use Share, then Add to Home Screen.
