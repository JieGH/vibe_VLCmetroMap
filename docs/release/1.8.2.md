# Release notes: Xarxa 1.8.2 (versionCode 9)

A small fix release on top of 1.8.1. No new features, so What's New does not pop up again.

## What's new for users

- **Menu, What's New, About and Feedback fit small and short screens.** Checked at 17 screen sizes (phones, landscape, a folded foldable, tablet, laptops) and at the largest text size. The About window's close button no longer slides off the edge on very narrow screens (280 px).
- On iPhone Safari with its toolbars showing, the expanded arrival sheets and the About / What's New windows are sized to the visible screen (shipped in the two commits before this one).

## Changes for the owner

- Wallet browsers (Brave, MetaMask) inject a script that throws `window.ethereum...` errors; these are no longer reported to Telegram as incidents (`reportIncident`, with a test).
- Version: `android/version.properties` (`versionCode=9`, `versionName=1.8.2`). What's New key unchanged (`1.8.1`).

## Not verified on a device

- Checked in headless Chromium with phone-sized windows, not on a real iPhone or Android phone, and Safari's address bar sliding away was not emulated.
- On landscape phones What's New and About still scroll in a small area (about 83 to 118 px); they work and close, but it is cramped. Left as is.
- No Android phone was connected, so the APK was built and signed but not installed.
