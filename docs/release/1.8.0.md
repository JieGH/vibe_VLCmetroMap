# Release notes: Xarxa 1.8.0 (versionCode 7)

A minor release on top of 1.7.0: bus times that no longer make you wait after a pin move, wider walk radii, and a friendlier opening view. Everything since 1.7.0 is `git log v1.7.0..HEAD`.

## What's new for users

- **Faster bus times after a pin move.** Each stop's times fill in as soon as they arrive, nearest first, instead of all at once after the slowest stop. Only the stops on screen are asked for; the rest load as you scroll. A tap on a stop was always instant; dropping or dragging the pin now feels the same (#144, #145).
- **A wave of dots while bus times load**, replacing the pulse and the bare `···`, on the split-view bus cards and the bus stop board.
- **Wider walk radii.** A pin or your location now shows metro within 450 m (was 320 m) and buses within 300 m (was 280 m). "Also within N min walk" shows 6 for metro.
- **The map opens closer** (zoom 12.7, was 12.3), so one tap drops a pin straight away; before, you had to zoom in first.
- **Station taps are a little stricter.** The snap distance around a station is 17.6 px (was 22 px), so a tap meant for the map is less likely to open a nearby station.
- Spanish and Valencian wording for the new What's New items.

## For the owner (not visible to users)

- **API trouble alerts to Telegram.** When EMT or FGV answers with a block or HTTP 429, or fails 5 times in a row, the app reports it through `/api/telemetry`; you get a message with the failure class, the requests that client sent in the last 10 s and 60 s, its IP and platform, and another message when the API recovers. One alert per API and failure class per 10 minutes. It also fires from your own device. This needs the deployed Netlify function and `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` (and optionally `TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID`) in Netlify. See ADR-0013.
- **EMT request budget** is now 24 requests per 6 s with a 1.5 s retry (was 12 per 10 s, 2.5 s); a pin drop fetches at most 4 stops and prefetches none (ADR-0013 follow-up). These numbers were chosen, not measured; the alerts above are how to find out whether EMT objects.

## Version places changed

- What's New: storage key `xarxa_whats_new_seen_1.8.0`; bullets "Faster Bus Times" and "Drop a Pin Right Away" in `WhatsNewModal.jsx`, en/es/ca dictionaries and the test. "Closer Nearby Stops" now says 450 m / 300 m.
- README: the metro-and-bus bullet; radii were already updated.
- Docs: ADR-0011, ADR-0012, ADR-0013, CONTEXT.md, AGENTS.md carry the new radii and budget.
- Privacy policy: not changed. The new alert sends only the API name, the failure class, request counts and the platform, and goes to the owner's own relay.

## Not verified on a device

- The bus-time fix: tested with a replay of the fetch policy and unit tests, not yet seen on a phone with real EMT answers.
- The wave dots' look and the 17.6 px tap radius.
- The Telegram alert has not fired for real: it needs the deploy and an actual refusal; test it with a hand-made POST to `/api/telemetry`.
- Spanish and Valencian wording has not been reviewed by a native speaker.
- #129 and #120 (animations and heat): the wave dots are a looping animation, transform and opacity only, and only while bus times are loading.

## Steps for the owner to publish

1. `git push origin main` (Cloudflare and Netlify deploy the web app and the alert function). Check the Telegram variables in Netlify.
2. `npm run build` (postbuild runs `npx cap sync ios`), then archive and upload from Xcode.
3. The APK for this version ships with 1.8.1 (see `docs/release/1.8.1.md`).
