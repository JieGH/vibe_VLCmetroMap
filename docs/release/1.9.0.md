# Release notes: Xarxa 1.9.0 (versionCode 9)

A minor release on top of 1.8.1: live buses on the map for EMT line 19. Everything since 1.8.1 is `git log v1.8.1..HEAD` (issues #146 to #153).

## What's new for users

- **Live line 19 buses on the map.** Select a stop on line 19 and the line's buses appear from their real GPS position, with a direction arrow, gliding along the route between updates.
- **Nearest approaching bus on the stop board**, with how many stops away it is, matched to the direction of the row you are looking at.
- **Tap a bus** to see its direction.
- **Line 19 only for now.** Positions refresh about every 30 s, and only while line 19 is focused; deselect it and the requests stop. No new tabs or screens.

## Changes for the owner

- Source: the Bus GPS Stream (ArcGIS Layer 384), one request per poll. Identity, `fecha` normalisation and heading follow ADR-0014 (accepted). ADR numbering is consistent (0001 to 0014, no gaps).
- Version: `android/version.properties` (`versionCode=9`, `versionName=1.9.0`) is the only version source for the APK. What's New key: `xarxa_whats_new_seen_1.9.0`, one new bullet ("Live Line 19 Buses") in en/es/ca. iOS keeps its Xcode marketing version.
- Privacy policy: not changed (a public municipal endpoint, no personal data sent).

## Known limits (developer-facing)

- The fleet layer can lag or omit a bus that the getSAE countdown already predicts (seen at stop 2314: getSAE 3 min, no bus in the layer). In that case "N stops away" is silently absent for that row.
- Live results were checked on 2026-10-29 against getSAE: stop 1627 showed 10 min vs 7 stops / 2.2 km; stop 1533 showed due vs 1 stop / 423 m.
- It has not been seen on a real map in a browser.

## Not verified

- Nobody has seen the buses on a real map yet: no browser or device session was available. Unit tests cover the tracker, `fecha` normalisation, the poller and the stop board match.
- The `fecha` offset in summer time (CEST) is derived, not observed; re-check on 2027-03-28 (ADR-0014).
- Spanish and Valencian wording has not been reviewed by a native speaker.

### Manual test checklist

1. Select a line 19 stop: buses appear within about 30 s, arrows point along the route, they move smoothly.
2. Open a line 19 stop board: nearest approaching bus and stops away show, and match the row's direction.
3. Tap a bus: its sheet shows the direction; the Android back button closes it.
4. Deselect line 19 (or pick a stop on another line): buses vanish and network requests to Layer 384 stop (devtools Network).
5. Select a non-19 stop: no bus requests at all.
6. Phone, landscape and desktop widths: no layout shift when the board slot fills.

3. `gh release create v1.9.0 --notes-file docs/release/1.9.0.md`, then `scripts/release-apk.sh v1.9.0` to attach the APK.
